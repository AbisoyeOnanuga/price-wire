# HF26 Week 2 — Side Hustle

Live. Due **2026-10-18, 11:59 PM PDT**. [Challenge page](https://dev.to/challenges/hacktoberfest-week2-2026-10-12).

A new project a specific person would pay for. The working core is an open-weight model, an open agent harness, or local inference. The post says why that beat a closed API (privacy, cost, offline, fine-tune, no lock-in).

**Win once.** Enter every category the product actually uses. Cash does not add up. Eligible surface = **$250** (overall) + each real category. Take-home is one prize: $250, $200, or $100. Target a surface of about **$750–$950**: one featured tool ($200) plus 3–5 partners ($300–$500). Writing quality outweighs a longer list.
Some partners like Render favour projects that use their services, if another partner has the same service or a different product is being used for the same service Render provides. I am unaware any other partner has this preference for their winners.
